# Industry diagnostics — v3.5.1_04

## Methodology
Industry diagnostics are loaded only when a supported market detail is opened. They do not slow the global refresh.

- United States: 11 liquid Select Sector ETF proxies aligned with GICS sectors (XLC, XLY, XLP, XLE, XLF, XLV, XLI, XLB, XLRE, XLK, XLU). These are proxies, not official S&P sector-index values.
- Croatia: official Zagreb Stock Exchange sector-index histories.
- Germany: transparent equal-weight baskets of major German listed companies, retained from prior releases.
- France, Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Denmark, Finland, Belgium and Poland: transparent equal-weight baskets of major locally listed companies grouped by economic industry.

Every industry shows 1M/3M trend, relative 3M performance, realized volatility, coverage/confidence, 60D driver correlation where available, a risk score, interpretation, diffusion summary and 6M normalized chart.
